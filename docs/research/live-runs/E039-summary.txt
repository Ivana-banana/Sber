The cliff is gone and the shape of what replaced it is the design working. Per-step
medians, control first:

  step   7     8     9    10    11    12    13    14
  E038   8.3  11.1  31.7  28.0  29.4   --    --    --
  E039   7.8  20.8  18.8  18.9   9.1   9.3   7.6   9.3

E038 pays a diverged prefix on every call from step 9 to the end of the task. E039 pays
one dear step around 8-10 and returns to the steady rate, which is what "one full
prefill when the budget overflows" predicts. It is a timing signature, not a logged
event -- the prompt is not written to the trace -- so it is evidence for the mechanism
and not a direct observation of the re-base.

Three of six prediction bands were missed, all in the same direction: the run is
cheaper than predicted. Steps 150-175 got 147, tokens per call 1350-1600 got 1293. The
history being a budget rather than a window is the reason: most tasks die before 8000
characters, so they never pay for the longer history at all, and the removed omission
counter takes a little off every call that used to carry it. The prediction treated the
budget as if it were always full.

The written criterion is in absolute seconds, which the day's own rule says are not
comparable between runs hours apart. It survives here only because the drift went the
wrong way for the factor: steady 2-6 rose 8.7 -> 9.6 s, so a slower stand still gave
31.6 -> 13.2. Normalised the drop is larger, 3.62 -> 1.38. The next prediction states
its criterion normalised.

Solved is unchanged and no task moved either way. Nothing about task-solving is claimed
from this run: what is confirmed is that the per-call cost of a long task stops growing.
E038's false win on fix-redirect-scheme did not recur, but that task timed out here
instead of reaching a verdict, so it is not evidence of a repaired class.
